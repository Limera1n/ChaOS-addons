# OpenMap map art

Converted from the installed OpenMap Forever addon with scripts/import-openmap.py.
Source: https://github.com/openmap-world/openmap

MapZones and MapArt preserve Blizzard Entertainment's original map art. Some gap fills in MapZones are by Santiago Reyes, credited by OpenMap (https://sreyesmaps.com/post/proyecto-atlas-de-azeroth-forever). These pictures are not covered by OpenMap's MIT software license.
